How it works

Each player walks their own map of ancient Jerusalem. Your Character Card lists the sites that person visited in Jesus’ final days. Visit every site on your card to complete the journey, then draw two new cards and choose one.

  • Each round one player rolls the die and everyone moves up to that many squares at the same time.
  • You only visit a site by finishing your move on it, so you can stop short to land exactly.
  • The game ends when someone has completed an Apostle, a Friend of Jesus and an Enemy of Christ. Most points wins.
