How it works

Match each prophet with the kings who reigned while he prophesied (or each king with his prophets). The longer the set, the more it scores. Every card says who it matches at the bottom; tap a card's magnifier to read it.

  • Draw until you hold 7 cards. Take from the draw pile, the top of any discard pile, or blindly from another player's hand, in any mix.
  • Play a prophet with a matching king to start a set, and add more matching cards to your sets (no card of the same name twice in a set).
  • Discard down to 5, which ends your turn.
  • The game ends when the last card is taken from the draw pile. Sets score 2 / 5 / 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 points for 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 cards.
