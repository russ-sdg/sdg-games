Prophets & Kings Scorepad Play online About the game

Playing at the table? When the game ends, tap + for each set a player built, by how many cards it has (the prophet or king on top counts). The scores add up as you go.

Scoring

Count both the prophet and king cards in a set. Add up every set a player has built; the most points wins.
