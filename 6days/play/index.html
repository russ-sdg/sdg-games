How it works

Everyone builds the same six days of creation, but every card you play scores for you. Each turn: draw a card (from the draw pile or the top of any discard pile), then play one card or discard it.

  • Start the next day with its own card: 2 points.
  • Add a day card to its own day or a later one that is started and not full: 1 point if that kind of creation is already there, 2 if it is new.
  • Close a full day with Evening & Morning: points equal to the day's number.
  • Day 1 needs 1 card, Day 2 needs 2, up to Day 6 needing 6. When every day is full the game ends and the most points wins.
