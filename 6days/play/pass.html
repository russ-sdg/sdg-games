How a turn goes

  1. The phone says who should have it. They tap I'm … — show me, and their hand appears.
  2. Draw a card from the draw pile or the top of a discard pile, then play one card on the days or discard it.
  3. The phone then asks you to pass it on. If you tapped the wrong card, the next screen has an Undo button until they pick the phone up.

Scoring is as in the box: 2 points to start a day or add a new kind of creature, 1 for a kind already there, and the day's number for closing a full day with Evening & Morning.
