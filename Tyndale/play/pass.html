Pass and play

You have a game in progress.

One phone, handed round the table. Before each turn the phone asks who should have it and hides the map until they say they are ready, so secrets stay secret.

Names
Who plays Tyndale?

With 4 or more Agents, one of the character cards is Tyndale's Secret Supporter. Each Agent is shown their own card in private at the start.

How a turn goes

  1. The phone says who should hold it. They tap Ready.
  2. An Agent rolls the die (1 to 3), taps where the pawn goes, and sees the two answer cards in private.
  3. They tell the table what they found — or, if they are the Secret Supporter, whatever they choose. What they report goes on the shared map.
  4. Tyndale (a person) plans a secret route; the map hides it again before the phone is passed on.
