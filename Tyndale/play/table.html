You have a table in progress.

This phone is the board and the referee. Each Bishop's Agent plays on their own phone (their map and scorecard), and you talk across the room. No account and no server.

Who plays Tyndale?

How it works

  1. Open the table. This phone shows a table code.
  2. Each Agent opens Join a table on their own phone and types the code. Everyone gets the same character card (one may be the Secret Supporter) and picks a name.
  3. Agents say where they place their pawns; the referee taps them in. Then Tyndale begins.
  4. On an Agent's turn they roll the die on their phone, move, and tell the referee where they landed. The referee shows the two answer cards to that Agent only, and the Agent tells the table what they found.
  5. The Agents keep track of the clues on their own phones. The referee phone keeps the real positions, Tyndale's shipments, and accusations.
