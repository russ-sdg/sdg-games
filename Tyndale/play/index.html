You have a game in progress.
How do you want to play?

With 4 or more Agents, one of the character cards is Tyndale's Secret Supporter. Someone may be holding it, but you won't know unless they are exposed.

How it works

  • Tyndale is translating the Bible into English. He must print each part at a new Printing Centre and ship it from a Coastal City to London.
  • Each turn you roll (1 to 3), move a pawn, and the game tells you whether Tyndale is there and whether he left a translation, or rumors of one, in that city.
  • Land on Tyndale and you win. If the Old Testament reaches London first, he does.
  • The clues he leaves form a trail: follow it to the end.
