Our story

We love to play games and, as homeschoolers, some of our favorite games are educational.

The idea for SDG Games grew out of our own desire to learn more about the Bible and what it teaches. There’s nothing better than to learn life-changing truths while having fun with your family and friends!

We launched our first game, Journeys with Jesus, in November 2020 so that we could better learn the geography of the Gospels. We have since developed a new lower-cost version of it, and added many more games, along with books and clothing.
