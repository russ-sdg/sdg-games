How it works here

The same rules as the board game. You and the computer players each hold Place Cards. Collect the cards along a road or boat ride between the two places on a Journey Card, then lay them down to score it.

Scripture Memory Tokens: at the start of your turn you may say a verse from one of your Place Cards from memory. You check yourself against the card, so it is on your honor. A token is worth 5 points if you keep it, or acts as a wild card.
