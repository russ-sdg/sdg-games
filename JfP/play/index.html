How it works

Paul is detained and wants news of the churches he planted. Send his co-workers Luke (starts in Philippi), Silas (Antioch) and Titus (Crete) to as many churches as you can.

  • The die gives a way to travel – Roman Road, Footpath or Boat – or Player's Choice.
  • The roller acts with one to three evangelists; every other player acts with one.
  • An evangelist moves along an unused route of that kind. If he can't move he can preach where he stands.
  • Preach in the same city again (after visiting two regions in the long game) and he is called as pastor – his journey ends.
  • The game ends when someone's three evangelists are all pastors, or nobody can travel.

Scoring

  • +4 for each region each evangelist has visited (the starting region counts)
  • +3 for each time an evangelist preached
  • +5 for each evangelist called as pastor
  • −2 for each city no evangelist visited
