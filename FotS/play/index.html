The box game has teams for four to six players. Online, you play on your own against up to three computer players.

How it works

Be the first to show all nine fruits of Galatians 5:22–23 on your Fruit Tree. Everyone holds five cards.

  1. Draw a card from the pile or the top of the discard pile — or skip your turn.
  2. Play one Fruit card by doing what it says. Joy and Faithfulness are extras.
  3. Play one Deed card if you like. It hurts an opponent, but it covers one of your own fruit.
  4. Discard what you do not need, then draw back up to five.

A covered fruit must be earned again, or redeemed by playing Self-Control (or when an opponent plays Love for you). A Wild card fills any gap when you cannot play another Fruit card.
