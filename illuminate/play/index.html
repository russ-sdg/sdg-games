How it works

Each hand, bid how many tricks you will win, then try to win exactly that many. Hands start at one card each and grow by one every hand.

  • Follow the suit (Gospel) that was led if you can. The highest card of that suit wins the trick.
  • Your role (Doctor, Neighbor, Preacher or Teacher) makes cards with your symbol Grace cards. Say “Grace!” and your card beats every card that is not Grace.
  • Four star cards change the rules for a trick: the lowest card wins, the leader wins, Grace is switched off, or the roles rotate.
  • Score the number you bid plus 2 if you take exactly that many tricks. In the 6- and 12-card hands you bid “all” or “nothing” for big points.
