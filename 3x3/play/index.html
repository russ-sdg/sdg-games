How it works

The deck has three cards for each of the thirteen tribes of Israel, numbered 1 to 13 in the order they set out from camp. Be the first to collect three sets of three matching cards, or all three Levi cards.

  • Everyone holds three cards, lined up from lowest to highest. Nine more lie face down in the matrix.
  • On your turn, turn over two cards, one at a time: flip a matrix card, or tap the highest or lowest card in anyone's hand (even yours).
  • If the two match, turn over a third. If all three match, they are yours. If not, every card goes back.
  • Remember what you see: you will need it. A hand's middle card is hidden unless you ask for the highest (or lowest) twice.
